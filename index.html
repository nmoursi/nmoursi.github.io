About

I am a master's student in Applied Artificial Intelligence at Northeastern University and a Research Assistant in the Human-Centered AI Lab, where I work with Prof. Dakuo Wang on human–AI interaction, AI-agent evaluation, adaptive learning, and the responsible deployment of AI-driven systems.

My current work studies how AI systems behave once they leave the lab: I lead the quantitative analysis for a CMU and U.S. Bank collaboration evaluating AI-driven quest personalization inside a live Roblox financial-literacy game, and I contribute qualitative coding and thematic analysis to research on LLM agents as simulated usability-testing participants.

Before graduate school I spent several years building products — most recently as a Product Manager at Microsoft across Skype and Microsoft Clarity — which is where my interest in measuring what systems actually do for the people using them began.

Research & Publications

Adaptive Learning in the Wild: A Randomized Trial of AI-Driven Quest Selection on Roblox

First author · Submitted to KDD 2027

A live randomized trial comparing fixed quest sequencing against Bayesian Knowledge Tracing–based personalization, evaluated across engagement, assessment performance, skill change, motivation, and reward participation. Analysis uses regression, interaction, subgroup, and robustness models on randomized experimental data.

UXAgent: Simulating Usability Testing of Web Design with LLM Agents as Participants

Co-author · Manuscript in progress

An LLM-agent system that lets UX researchers dry-run usability studies with simulated participants interacting with live websites. I contribute qualitative coding and thematic analysis of participant behavior, trust, and perceptions of AI-agent systems.
